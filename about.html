About MYPickleSpot

Malaysia's largest pickleball court directory — 573 venues across 14 states.

What We Do

MYPickleSpot aggregates pickleball venues across Malaysia into one searchable directory. We help players find available courts, check real-time slots, and discover new places to play — from Kuala Lumpur to Kota Kinabalu.

Our Data

We currently list 573 venues across 14 states and territories, sourced from OpenCourt365 (190 venues), PicklePadel.my (379 venues), PlayByPoint (3 venues), and CourtOS (1 venue). Every venue includes location coordinates, contact info, and amenities where available.

Real-Time Availability

For venues integrated with OpenCourt365, you can search actual court availability by date and time — see open slots and prices without having to call around.
