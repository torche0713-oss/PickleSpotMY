About PickleSpotMY

Malaysia's largest pickleball court directory.

What We Do

PickleSpotMY aggregates pickleball venues across Malaysia into one searchable directory. We help players find available courts, check real-time slots, and discover new places to play — from Kuala Lumpur to Kota Kinabalu.

Our Data

We currently list 398 venues across 14 states and territories, sourced from multiple platforms including OpenCourt365 and PicklePadel.my. Every venue includes location coordinates, contact info, and amenities where available.

Real-Time Availability

For venues integrated with OpenCourt365, you can search actual court availability by date and time — see open slots and prices without having to call around.
